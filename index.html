Who We Are

AWKN Within is a spiritual community gathered around one conviction: that the Divine Creator does not reside in some distant place, but within each of us. Our name proclaims our purpose: to carry wisdom that is ancient into a form that can be known, understood, and lived now.

We gather in sacred ceremony on ranch land in the Texas Hill Country: in prayer, in song, in silence, and in sacrament, guided by the medicine carriers and lineages we hold in the highest honor.

We are not a retreat center, a clinic, or a wellness brand. We are a sanctuary: small by intention, bound by relationship, and open to those who feel called to walk with us.

Our Statement of Beliefs

What We Believe

Inward

The inward journey is the most direct path we know to communion with the Divine. In the sacred states our ceremonies open, attention turns within, and what has long been held in the subconscious rises to be witnessed, explored, and understood.

Release

A human life accumulates layers of conditioning: fears, roles, wounds, and inherited beliefs. In ceremony these layers can be seen for what they are and released. What remains is the soul itself, and with it a remembering of why we came here.

Evolution

We are here for the evolution of our souls. Our sacraments do not perform this work for us; they reveal how it is to be done. Our purpose is to hold sacred and safe space in which that evolution can unfold, and to walk alongside one another as it does.

Belonging

We are not visitors to the living world, but members of it. Modern life asks a slow forgetting of that kinship; ceremony is where it is remembered. We honor the elements that make and sustain us: earth beneath us, water within us, air moving through us, fire at the center of the circle. In their company the body settles into a rhythm older than our worrying, and remembers it was never separate from the greater life that holds it.

Lineage

This wisdom is ancient, and it was never lost, only forgotten. We honor the indigenous lineages and ancestors who carried these practices faithfully across generations so they would survive to reach us. This wisdom is not a relic to be studied from a distance, but a living knowing to be practiced now.

Our Practices

The Ways We Gather

Every gathering is held within the life of the congregation, prepared for beforehand, integrated afterward, and open to our members. We honor the solstices and equinoxes with reverence, as our ancestors did.

Monthly · One Evening

Community Ceremony

The heartbeat of our community. An evening of shared ceremony, prayer, and song on the land, closed with a simple meal and a morning sharing circle. This is where community is made, and where new members first sit in circle.

Multi-Day · On the Land

Sacred Retreats

Retreats of several days for members ready to go deeper, held in a safe and carefully prepared container: preparation calls and meetings before arrival, ceremony and reflection on the land, shared meals, and organized integration afterward to support what opens during the retreat.

By Invitation

Private Ceremony & Counsel

For seasons of life that ask for undivided attention, such as grief, transition, or purpose, we offer private, held ceremony and counsel with our ministers, shaped entirely around one member's walk.

Monthly · Ongoing

Integration Circles

Insight that is not lived is not yet wisdom. Our monthly integration meetings help members carry what was received in ceremony into the ordinary hours of their lives, the place where the ancient becomes known now.

The Container

How We Hold Ceremony

Ceremony at AWKN Within follows the ancient form: an opening prayer to the directions and of protection, the blessing and serving of the sacrament, and prayer and song under the direction of the medicine carrier leading the circle.

Before

Preparation

Every participant is known to us before ceremony. Pre-retreat calls and meetings with our ministry prepare each person for what is ahead: a written wellbeing screening, an intention conversation, and guidance for the days leading in. An intention is not a demand placed upon the medicine. It is an honest question carried into ceremony.

During

Watchful Care

Facilitators remain present and attentive throughout, and at all times at least one remains awake, sober, and observing. No one departs during ceremony, and no sacrament ever leaves the ceremonial space.

After

Integration

The morning sharing circle begins the true work. Organized integration follows every retreat: calls and circles in the weeks after, so the experiences that happen in ceremony are supported, understood, and carried into daily life.

Those Who Hold the Space

Our Facilitators

The living bridge between the ancient and the now.

The facilitators who hold our circles are experienced in working with the sacraments, formed by years of practice, study, and their own walks with the medicine. They serve with reverence, and they carry deep respect for the indigenous practices and lineages from which this work descends.

We do not treat these traditions as techniques to borrow. We honor the medicine carriers, the medicine men and women whose knowledge reached us through generations of faithful keeping, and we hold their guidance at the center of how ceremony is conducted here. Every facilitator who holds space at AWKN Within is entrusted with that responsibility personally, and carries it as ministry, not as a role.

The Land

Where We Gather

We practice on working ranch land in the Hill Country: under oak and cedar, beside the fire circle, within the temple and the dome. The land is not a backdrop to our ceremonies; it is a member of them. We tend it, we quiet ourselves on it, and we leave it better than we found it.

Our ceremonies are conducted under the guidance of the medicine men and women whose lineages we honor as the living bridge between the ancient and the now.

The Path

Becoming a Member

Ceremony at AWKN Within is a practice of membership, not a purchase. We come to know each person before we sit in circle together, for your safety and for the integrity of the container we all share.

i

Write to Us

Tell us, in your own words, what draws you toward this work and toward community. There is no application to perform. Sincerity is the only requirement.

ii

An Intention Conversation

A member of our ministry will sit with you, by phone or on the land, to hear your story, share how we practice, and walk through our preparation and wellbeing screening together.

iii

Enter the Circle

New members are welcomed first at a community ceremony evening. From there, our deeper practices open in their own time, at the pace of trust, not the pace of a calendar.

Contribution

How This Community Is Carried

Our gatherings are sustained by member contributions, offered in the spirit of reciprocity. No one is turned from ceremony for lack of means. Contribution levels and our scholarship circle are shared with members during the intention conversation, never posted like a menu.

Giving

Support the Mission

Friends and patrons sustain what contributions alone cannot: the land we practice on, the scholarship circle that keeps our doors open to all, and the quiet work of tending this community. If you feel called to give, write to us. We would love to talk.

If something in you already knows, write to us.

We grow by attraction, not promotion. The greatest testimony to this work is the healing and the change in those who practice with us.
